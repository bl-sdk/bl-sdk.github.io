On-foot movement tuning: sprint/walk speed, jump, gravity, glide, friction, and vault costs.

Mods menu sliders and presets, or console (`player_move_help`, `player_move_apply`, presets `fast` / `moon` / `glide_up`). Optional BLImGui tab. Scope picker: local / all / others.

Install `bl4_player_movement.sdkmod` in `sdk_mods`. Re-apply after travel if sliders stop sticking.
