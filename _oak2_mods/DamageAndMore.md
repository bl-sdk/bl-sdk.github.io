Combat tuning for Borderlands 4: damage dealt/taken, elemental output, repair kits, shields, Second Wind, melee, and related sliders.

Mods menu + console (`bdam_help`, `bdam_apply`). Optional **BLImGui** tab — install [BLImGui](https://github.com/juso40/blimgui), bind **Open Damage & More tab** (Ctrl+Shift+F11). Sticky re-apply optional.

Install `damage_and_more.sdkmod` in `sdk_mods`. Client-side.
