Co-op party roster and player teleports for Borderlands 4.

Mods menu + **BLImGui** tab (install [BLImGui](https://github.com/juso40/blimgui), bind **Open P2P Teleporter tab**) or console (`bcst_roster`, `bcst_to_me`, `bcst_me_to`). Host authority may be required.

Install `p2p_teleporter.sdkmod` in `sdk_mods`. MIT license.
