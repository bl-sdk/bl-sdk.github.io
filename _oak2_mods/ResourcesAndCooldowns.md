Resource and recovery sliders: repair kit charges/cooldown, overshield, Second Wind, lifesteal, ammo regen.

Mods menu + console (`brc_help`, `brc_apply`). Optional **BLImGui** tab — [BLImGui](https://github.com/juso40/blimgui), bind **Open Resources & Cooldowns tab** (Ctrl+Shift+F12).

Install `resources_and_cooldowns.sdkmod` in `sdk_mods`. Works standalone or alongside Damage & More.
