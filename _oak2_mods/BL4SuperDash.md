Perform Borderlands 4's Super Dash movement technique with one rebindable key or controller button. Works from a standstill, while moving forward, and while sprinting; preserves sprint after landing when started from sprint. Supports keyboard/mouse and gamepad controls and uses native Dash/Jump calls without external macro software. Client-side co-op compatible.
