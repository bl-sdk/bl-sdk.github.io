Mob and IO spawn catalog with aggro modes, mix spawns, and favorites for Borderlands 4.

**BLImGui** tabs (install [BLImGui](https://github.com/juso40/blimgui), **Ctrl+F6** or F1 → BMS) plus console (`bms_spawn`, `bms_help`). **Requires Oak Spawner** enabled separately.

Install `mob_spawner.sdkmod` in `sdk_mods`. MIT license.
