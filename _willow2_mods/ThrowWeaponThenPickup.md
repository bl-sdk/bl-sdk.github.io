Throw your current weapon and pick it back up immediately, using internal functions.

Press `H` (default). The well-known technique gives you:

1. Instant reload with no reloading animation - Gaige can reload this way too.
2. Instant weapon swap with no holstering animation, e.g. sniper into Lady Fist, Flakker into Ahab, Sand Hawk + Bee into Amigo Sincero.

The keybind is configurable in the mod options.
