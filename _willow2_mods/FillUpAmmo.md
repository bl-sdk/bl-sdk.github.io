Refill all of your ammo at an ammo vending machine with a single keybind.

- Refill all of your ammo at the ammo vending machine with the "Secondary Use" key.
- Every ammo type can be toggled on or off individually in the mod options.
- If you do not have enough money, it buys as much as it can.

Works in BL2 and TPS. In multiplayer both the host and the client need the mod.
