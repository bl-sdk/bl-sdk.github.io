Make the side missions that reward farmable gear repeatable.

Supported rewards: Fibber, Pimpernel, Swordsplosion, Kitten, Heartbreaker, Rubi, Lady Fist, Amigo Sincero, Hail, Creamer, Chere-amie, Orc, Love Thumper, and Sand Hawk. Treasure of the Sands and Creature Slaughter: Round 2 are supported as well.

Every mission can be toggled individually in the mod options.

Sand Hawk details: the mission chain Washburne Refinery / C3n50r807 / I Know It When I See It has its reward changed to the Sand Hawk, the mission becomes repeatable, and `Don't Copy That Floppy` no longer requires completing this mission first.
