Brings Borderlands 3's gun mechanics to Borderlands 2, and adds two elements BL2 never had: **radiation** (from BL3) and **cryo** (from The Pre-Sequel).

**Gun mechanics** (one Weapon Mode key, default B):
- **Maliwan** guns swap to a second element, picked from fire, shock, corrosive, slag, cryo and radiation.
- **Vladof** assault rifles deploy an underbarrel launcher.
- **Torgue** guns toggle between impact and sticky rounds.
- **Dahl** guns cycle full-auto, burst and semi-auto.
- **Tediore** guns roll a BL3 reload throw (turret, MIRV, chaser, bouncing).
- **Hyperion** guns raise a frontal energy shield while aiming, **Jakobs** critical hits ricochet to a nearby enemy, **Bandit** guns overheat instead of reloading.
- Optional BL3 **anointments** on rare and better guns, BL3 artifact (relic) perks, close-range sniper sights, loot beams by rarity, and item cards that show all of it.
- **HeartShatter**, the mod's own legendary Vladof sniper. Console: `heartshatter give`.

**Radiation:** a damage-over-time element. An irradiated enemy hurts the ones around it and explodes when it dies, leaving a cloud. Radiation guns, grenade mods and shields drop in the world at rates balanced against the other elements (plain guns 6%, E-tech and Maliwan guns 16%, legendaries 8%; one-element and no-element legendaries such as Maggie and Hornet never roll it), with their own look: yellow muzzle flashes and tracers, gas explosions with rays, card icons and names. Console: `radiation give`, `radiation apply`.

**Cryo:** freezing and shattering, cryo guns, grenades, shields and relics that drop like any other element, the frozen-enemy look and the snowflake icon.

**Requirements and notes**
- Radiation's gas, bubbles and smoke need the **Willow Texture Loader (texloader)**; without it they are not drawn (everything else still works).
- Cryo's ice effects, icon and texts are built on your PC from your own copy of **Borderlands: The Pre-Sequel**; nothing of the game ships. Without it cryo guns still drop and work, without those effects. Radiation does not need The Pre-Sequel.
- The Geiger-counter sound of radiation explosions needs the optional **blaudio** mod.
- The **Aurelia** mod is not required; when it is installed, it supplies cryo and everything else keeps working.
- Cryo is built on the cryo element of Grim Reader's Aurelia mod, used with permission. Co-op needs every player to have the mod; it has not been tested.
