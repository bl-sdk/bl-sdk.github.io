Displays a configurable timer on your HUD that counts the seconds before your shield starts to recharge.

Feel free to contact me with any problems or suggestions via [Discord](https://discord.gg/bXeqV8Ef9R) @LaryIsland.

## Options
- **Timer Position**
- **Timer Size**
- **Text Colour:** RGBA for the timer text.
- **Font:** choose between five of the game's fonts.
- **Enable Movement Keys:** move the timer with the arrow keys and resize it with the scroll wheel.
- **Show in Pause Menu:** keep the timer visible while the game is paused.
- **Only when Depleted:** only show the timer once your shield is fully depleted.
- **Only with Roid Shield:** only show the timer while a roid shield is equipped.

## Co-op
Works fully client-side.
