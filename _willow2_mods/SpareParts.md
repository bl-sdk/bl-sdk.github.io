Salvage parts from items and attach them to other items.

If using the mod in Insane mode, also install
[Sanity Saver]({{ "/willow2-mod-db/mods/sanitysaver/" | relative_url }}), otherwise **your guns will be deleted**.

To find out what individual parts do, head over to [apple1417](https://github.com/apple1417)'s [bl2.parts](https://bl2.parts/).

Feel free to contact me with any problems or suggestions via [Discord](https://discord.gg/bXeqV8Ef9R) @LaryIsland.

## Usage Guide
1. Open your backpack and select an item with [Enter] or [E].
2. Hover the item you want to salvage parts from, and press the 'Salvage' hotkey [C].
3. A popup will show you the parts the mod found, and if they're compatible with the chosen item.
4. Choose which parts you want, and inspect the result with [F].
5. Confirm with the 'Salvage' hotkey [C], or press [Escape] to cancel.

## Options
By default Spare Parts blocks certain actions to provide a more balanced experience.
Each of these can be toggled individually in the settings.

### Parts Transfer Method
- **Regular:** the item you salvage parts from is scrapped in the process.
- **Swap:** the two items exchange parts, and you keep both.

### Rarity Lock
You can only salvage parts from an equal or higher rarity item.

i.e. Blue items can receive from Blue/Purple/Legendary items, but not White/Green items.

### Strict Uniques
Unique items can only receive from other copies of the same unique item.

i.e. An Unkempt Harold can only use parts from another Unkempt Harold.

### Sanity Check Safeguard
- **Safe:** every combination will pass the sanity check. Certain relic/class mod parts are not salvageable.
- **Expert:** if combined incorrectly, some relic/class mod combinations will not pass the sanity check.
- **Insane:** all safeguards are removed.

## Co-op
Reported to work in Borderlands 2 co-op, but not in the Pre-Sequel.
