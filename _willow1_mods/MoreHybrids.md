Adds 8 new hybrids to the game that were originally prevented from spawning. These will normally get deleted on a game restart but as long as this is installed they wont get deleted.

The Guns:

Knoxxs Gold Gemini

Lady Finger Invader

Athenas Wisdom Invader

Typhoon Wildcat

Chopper Revolution

Jackal Bulldog

Avenger Guardian

Bessie Skullmasher

and adds the dive hornet back in on goty enhanced.


Installation:
Drop the MoreHybrids.sdkmod file in Borderlands/sdk_mods or BorderlandsGOTYEnhanced/sdk_mods and enable it with the in game mods menu.
