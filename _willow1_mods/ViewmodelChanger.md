Sets where your gun sits on screen and its field of view, for each gun type.

## Settings

- **Reset to Default**
- **Pistols**, **Revolvers**, **SMGs**, **Combat Rifles**, **Shotguns**, **Sniper Rifles**,
  **Launchers** and **Eridian Weapons**, each with Gun FOV, Forward and Back, Left and Right,
  Up and Down.
