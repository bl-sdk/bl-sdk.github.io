# Minimap

Backports the Borderlands 1 Game of the Year enhanced to regular GOTY.

## Settings

- **Show compass**: Show or hide the original compass independently of the minimap. Ported from enhanced
- **Show minimap**: Show or hide the minimap. Ported from enhanced
- **Rotate minimap**: Rotate the map with the player. When off, the map stays north-up and the player arrow rotates. Ported from enhanced
- **Minimap zoom**: Set zoom between 100% and 1000%. Higher values show a smaller area.
