Removes the flashes and sparks from gunfire, yours and enemies', each with its own setting.

Every setting starts on Disable, which hides that effect. Enable brings it back.

## Settings

- **Weapon Muzzle**: the flame at the barrel when a gun fires.
- **Weapon Flicker**: the light that blinks on the walls with each shot.
- **Gun Explosions**: the small burst an elemental bullet sets off where it lands.
- **Bullet Decals**: the sparks where a bullet lands.
- **Bullet Trails**: the glowing dots an elemental bullet leaves on its way.
